## What is it

People draw different conclusions from the exact same information depending on how it is presented (positive vs negative frame).

## Why it matters

Word choice and visual context fundamentally alter perceived value and risk. Framing a $10 monthly discount as *"Save $120 a year"* feels far more substantial to users, driving higher conversion without changing underlying pricing.

## Key ideas

- **Gain Framing vs Loss Framing**: Highlighting what users gain (*"95% Success Rate"*) inspires confidence; highlighting loss (*"5% Failure Rate"*) triggers anxiety.
- **Contextual Anchoring**: The surrounding copy and visuals set the emotional lens through which data is evaluated.
- **Attribute Framing**: Emphasizing a single positive product attribute elevates total perceived quality.

## How it works

Information Lens Transformation:

$$\text{Objective Fact} \xrightarrow[\text{Gain Frame}]{\text{Positive Lens}} \text{High Perceived Value} \quad \text{vs} \quad \text{Objective Fact} \xrightarrow[\text{Loss Frame}]{\text{Risk Lens}} \text{Anxiety / Caution}$$

## Example

1. **SaaS Subscription Copy**: Framing pricing as *"Save 20% by paying annually"* (Positive Gain Frame) versus *"Monthly plan incurs a 20% surcharge"* (Negative Penalty Frame).
2. **Security App Messaging**: Framing cloud backups as *"Protect 10,000 precious family photos from loss"* (Loss Avoidance Frame) versus *"100GB Cloud Storage Included"* (Feature Frame).

## Application

- **Frame Onboarding Benefits Around User Outcomes**: Use gain-framed value statements (*"Gain 5 hours back every week"*).
- **Frame System Warnings Around Risk Prevention**: Use loss-avoidance copy for security confirmations (*"Enable 2FA to prevent unauthorized account access"*).
- **Reframe Pricing Per Day**: Frame annual subscriptions as *"$0.80 / day"* to lower perceived financial commitment.

## Limitations

- **Deceptive Framing & Brand Erosion**: Using manipulative framing to disguise hidden fees or misleading statistics destroys brand reputation.
- **Skeptical Audiences**: B2B enterprise procurement buyers evaluate raw financial specs, ignoring superficial marketing framing.

## Common misconceptions

- **"Framing changes facts"**: Framing never alters factual data; it alters cognitive focus and emotional evaluation.
- **"Positive framing is ALWAYS better"**: For safety, health, and security products, negative loss-avoidance framing often outperforms positive gain framing.

## Using AI

**Where it helps**
- Generating A/B test variations of landing page copy framed around gains, loss avoidance, and social proof.
- Analyzing customer support transcripts to detect negative emotional framing patterns.

**Where it fails**
- Evaluating true audience emotional trust and resonance across specialized industry domains.
