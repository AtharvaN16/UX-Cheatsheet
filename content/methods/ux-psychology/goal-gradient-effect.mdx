## What is it

Users accelerate their effort and velocity as they get closer to achieving a goal.

## Why it matters

Motivation is not constant throughout a workflow. By providing clear visual progress indicators and artificial initial progress (the endowed progress effect), completion rates increase significantly.

## Key ideas

- **Accelerated Motivation**: The closer a user perceives themselves to the finish line, the faster they work to complete the remaining steps.
- **Endowed Progress Effect**: Giving users artificial credit toward a goal upfront (e.g., a 10-stamp loyalty card pre-stamped twice) yields faster completion than an un-stamped 8-stamp card.
- **Visual Feedback**: Clear progress bars transform abstract effort into tangible proximity to completion.

## How it works

Perceived proximity vs effort curve:

```
User Velocity / Motivation
        │                                  /  [GOAL COMPLETED]
        │                                 /
        │                                /
        │                      /─────────
        │             /────────
        └─────────────┴────────────────────────────►
            Step 1        Step 2       Step 3 (Near Goal)
```

## Example

LinkedIn's profile strength meter awards users immediate progress (e.g., "50% Complete!") simply for creating an account, which motivates users to add skills and education to reach "100% All-Star Status."

## Application

- **Endow Initial Progress**: Start progress indicators at 10–20% complete rather than 0% on step 1 of onboarding.
- **Display Clear Steps**: Use numbered progress bars (e.g., "Step 3 of 4") so users realize how close they are to finishing.
- **Chunk Long Forms**: Break 15 input fields into 3 short steps to show rapid movement through early stages.

## Limitations

- **Post-Goal Slump**: Immediately after reaching a goal, user motivation drops sharply (post-completion fatigue).
- **False Progress Backlash**: If users realize artificial progress was misleading or that hidden surprise steps exist, trust is lost.

## Common misconceptions

- **"Completion meters work for everything"**: Showing a progress bar on routine daily actions creates unnecessary visual noise.
- **"0% progress is honest so it's best"**: Starting at 0% activates cold-start friction, whereas framing registration as Step 1 of 4 endows immediate progress.

## Using AI

**Where it helps**
- Analyzing onboarding funnels to determine optimal step counts and progress placement.
- Generating motivational microcopy for milestone completion screens.

**Where it fails**
- Determining if users feel manipulated by aggressive gamification or progress meters.
