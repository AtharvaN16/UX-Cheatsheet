## What is it

Specifies that behavior is generated by continuous interaction between Capability, Opportunity, and Motivation.

## Why it matters

When users fail to adopt a complex product (e.g., failing to adhere to a digital health tracking regimen or budget planning tool), product teams often blame user laziness. COM-B provides a rigorous diagnostic framework to identify whether the breakdown is caused by lack of knowledge (Capability), environmental barriers (Opportunity), or emotional alignment (Motivation).

## Key ideas

- **Capability**: Psychological (knowledge, comprehension) and physical (skills, stamina) capacity to perform the activity.
- **Opportunity**: Physical (time, location, tools) and social (cultural norms, peer support) environment enabling the behavior.
- **Motivation**: Reflective (conscious evaluation, goals) and automatic (habits, emotional impulses) mental processes directing action.

## How it works

Interlocking Behavioral System:

```
          ┌─────────────────────────┐
          │     CAPABILITY (C)      │
          └────────────┬────────────┘
                       │
                       ▼
┌──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┐
│           MOTIVATION (M)                   │ ──► [ BEHAVIOR (B) ]
└──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┘
                       ▲
                       │
          ┌────────────┴────────────┐
          │     OPPORTUNITY (O)     │
          └─────────────────────────┘
```

## Example

1. **Digital Health App Adoption**: A diabetes tracking app fails if users lack **Psychological Capability** (don't understand blood glucose numbers), **Physical Opportunity** (don't own a Bluetooth glucometer), or **Automatic Motivation** (feel anxious when opening the app).
2. **Fintech Investment Apps (Robinhood)**: Simplifies **Psychological Capability** with visual charts, provides **Physical Opportunity** via fractional shares starting at $1, and drives **Reflective Motivation** via goal tracking.

## Application

- **Conduct Behavioral Barrier Audits**: Map user interview findings into Capability, Opportunity, and Motivation codes to pinpoint product drop-off causes.
- **Enhance Psychological Capability**: Provide inline tooltips, micro-learning cards, and clear data visualizer tools.
- **Expand Physical Opportunity**: Build cross-device sync, offline modes, and one-click integration connections.

## Limitations

- **Academic Complexity**: COM-B is more comprehensive and requires more research synthesis effort than lighter industry models like BJ Fogg's $B=MAP$.
- **Systemic Environment Constraints**: If Opportunity barriers are external real-world regulatory laws, software UI design alone cannot solve adoption.

## Common misconceptions

- **"Motivation is separate from Capability"**: Increasing user knowledge (Capability) directly boosts user confidence and reflective Motivation.
- **"Opportunity just means UI buttons"**: Opportunity includes social norms, team culture, physical hardware availability, and available time.

## Using AI

**Where it helps**
- Categorizing qualitative user interview transcripts into Capability, Opportunity, and Motivation diagnostic buckets automatically.
- Recommending behavior change intervention strategies based on identified COM-B barrier scores.

**Where it fails**
- Conducting complex field observation research in specialized healthcare or enterprise contexts.
