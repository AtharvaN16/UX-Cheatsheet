## What is it

A quantitative and a qualitative strand planned together to answer one question, with an explicit decision about which leads and where the datasets meet. The plan for that meeting *is* the design: a survey and some interviews run in parallel and never integrated is two studies, not mixed methods.

## Purpose

To give each strand a different job. Quant establishes prevalence and magnitude; qual establishes mechanism, meaning and vocabulary. The design makes the handoff deliberate, so the second strand is scoped *from* the first's results.

## When to use

- A metric moved and nobody can explain it.
- You are about to measure something you cannot yet name, so there is no instrument to write.
- A decision needs both a size and a reason.
- Different stakeholders discount different evidence and the case must survive both.

## Structure

Creswell and Plano Clark's three core designs differ only in sequence and priority.

### Convergent parallel

Both strands run at once, then merge in a joint display setting each theme beside the number it speaks to. Equal priority; viable only when both can be specified up front.

### Explanatory sequential

Quant leads. You find a surprising result, then sample qualitatively *from inside it* — participants chosen from the first strand's data, which is what makes it sequential rather than two parallel studies. A fresh convenience sample throws the mechanism away.

### Exploratory sequential

Qual leads. You discover the constructs, categories and wording, then build an instrument from them and measure how common they are. The qual output is an *input* to the quant design.

## How to use

- Write the single question both strands serve. If it splits cleanly in two, you have two studies.
- Choose sequence and priority explicitly; it is the only decision here that changes what you learn.
- Name the integration point before fieldwork — joint display, sampling frame, coded matrix. Fetters, Curry and Creswell place integration at the design, methods and reporting levels.
- Decide in advance what a disagreement means; divergence is a result, and teams without a rule believe whichever strand suits them.

## Example

An invoicing product saw trial-to-paid conversion fall after a signup redesign while every funnel step held or improved (figures illustrative). Explanatory sequential: quant defined the population — trials that finished onboarding then went dormant — and nine interviews drawn *only* from that cohort found "invite your accountant" had moved behind a settings menu, and the accountant usually authorises the purchase. Integration artefact: a joint display pairing each dormant segment with its stated reason.

## Common mistakes

- **Two parallel studies called mixed methods.** Analysed separately, written up in sequence, nothing integrated — nothing gained over either alone.
- **Sequential in name, parallel in fact.** The second strand was recruited before the first was analysed, so its sample cannot speak to the result.
- **No protocol for disagreement.** When the survey says satisfied and the interviews say frustrated, an unprepared team quietly drops one.
- **Treating the qual strand as a small quant strand.** Nine interviews do not establish prevalence, however carefully you count who said what.

## Limitations

- **Cost is additive; the inference is not.** Integration can still produce nothing neither strand had alone.
- **Sequential designs serialise the schedule.** The second strand cannot be briefed until the first is analysed.
- **Integration is a judgement call.** No statistic tells you how far a theme should move your reading of a number.
- **It cannot rescue a weak strand.** A badly worded survey integrated with good interviews yields a well-structured wrong answer.

## Using AI

**Where it helps**
- Drafting the joint display that puts each theme next to the metric it claims to explain.
- Turning an exploratory strand's codes into candidate survey items, flagging the double-barrelled ones.

**Where it fails**
- Performing the integration. Asked to reconcile themes with metrics, a model produces a fluent narrative in which they agree — smoothing the divergence this design exists to expose, a consensus manufactured from two summaries.
