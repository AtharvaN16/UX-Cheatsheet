## What is it
People sort topics into groups that make sense to them and name those groups.

## Purpose
Tells you how people naturally group your content before you build a structure around it.

## When to use
- You are building a structure and need to know how people group things

## How to do it
1. Write each topic on its own card

## Common mistakes
- **Forcing your own categories onto the results** — let the groupings emerge

## Tips
Run it before you draft a navigation.

## Using AI
**Where it helps**
- Cluster raw sort data into candidate groupings for review

**Where it fails**
- Naming the final categories without checking participant labels
